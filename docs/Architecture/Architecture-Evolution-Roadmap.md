# Architecture Evolution Roadmap

Status: Phase 4.6 governance baseline.

The Aether roadmap is organized into Eras. Eras describe architectural maturity,
not marketing milestones.

## Era I: Foundation

Status: completed.

Scope:

- monorepo foundation;
- Core Runtime;
- Kernel;
- Service Platform;
- Official Service Map;
- Core System Services;
- Kernel Decomposition;
- Aether Engineering Protocol;
- Architecture Constitution v2.

Outcome:

The platform now has stable architectural boundaries, governance, validation,
and checkpoint discipline.

## Era II: Cognitive Core

Status: architecture and design active; implementation not started.

Approved cognitive-domain sequence:

- Memory;
- Knowledge;
- Context;
- Planning;
- Reasoning.
- Decision;
- Learning;
- Perception.

Memory, Knowledge, and Context have accepted and published architecture
documents. Planning is the next reserved documentation phase. No cognitive
domain has runtime implementation.

Rule:

Era II must not bypass Domains, Services, Managers, Policies, ASB, or Contract
Bus contracts.

## Era III: Intelligence

Status: future.

Planned areas:

- LLM;
- Inference;
- Agents;
- Skills;
- Prompt Engine.

Rule:

Era III must not introduce AI execution without policy, telemetry, capability,
permission, and resource contracts.

## Era IV: Automation

Status: future.

Planned areas:

- Workflow;
- Desktop;
- Browser;
- Integrations.

Rule:

Era IV must use Driver and Policy boundaries for any external or user-visible
action.

## Era V: Enterprise

Status: future.

Planned areas:

- Licensing;
- Billing;
- Compliance;
- Marketplace.

Rule:

Era V must not weaken privacy, auditability, tenant isolation, or policy
enforcement for commercial convenience.

## Era VI: Distributed

Status: future.

Planned areas:

- Cluster;
- Cloud;
- Synchronization;
- Federation.

Rule:

Era VI must preserve local-first contracts and introduce distributed concerns
behind explicit transport and consistency boundaries.
